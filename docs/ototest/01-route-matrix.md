“Component” means a Vue/Vitest assertion; it does not prove browser history,
real API persistence, or visual layout. A `gap` row may have some assertions,
but at least one behavior remains unproven at the required evidence layer. All
unresolved route gaps are owned by Knowledge Base maintainers. `/development`
is temporary tooling and excluded from supported route completeness.

Each route row has one overall evidence status. A `gap` status means the
complete route behavior in that row still has unresolved evidence listed in
the final column; a `covered` status applies only when the row's behavior is
asserted at the stated evidence layer.
